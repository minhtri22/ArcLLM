# ARCLLM_TTFT_M1 — P4 Falsification and STOP Contract

**Date:** 2026-09-22  
**Status:** frozen before implementation.

## Purpose

P4 prevents a TTFT study from turning into an optimization loop. A mechanism is either supported by its frozen contrast, falsified by it, or left unresolved. No failed gate creates permission to invent another arm or tune a threshold.

## Adjudication order

F0 provenance/environment/freshness → F1 H-ART static identity → F2 semantic/structural validity → F3 session/order validity → F4 H-DPIPE → F5 H-PRECOND → F6 state interaction → F7 parent-like reproduction/H-NULL → final mechanism classification.

## H-ART hard STOP

If any common prefill SPIR-V differs: `H_ART_SUPPORTED_STATIC` and `STOP_TIMING`. The factorial timing study is unnecessary until artifact identity is normalized.

If every common prefill SPIR-V matches: `H_ART_FALSIFIED_STATIC`. Only then may a separately authorized fresh factorial run proceed.

## Materiality

All causal support gates use the already-existing prospective magnitude `1.10`. No threshold is derived from the historical parent P6 ratios. Workloads and sessions are adjudicated independently; pooling cannot turn a failed cell into PASS.

## Mechanism rules

**H-DPIPE:** `QP/SP >= 1.10` in W-S and W-C in both sessions.

**H-PRECOND:** `QF/QP >= 1.10` and `SF/SP < 1.10` in both workloads and both sessions. This prevents a common warmup effect from being mislabeled as the cause of candidate/reference divergence.

**State interaction:** `QF/SF >= 1.10` and `(QF/SF)/(QP/SP) >= 1.10` in both workloads and both sessions.

**H-NULL:** after H-ART is falsified, `QF/SF < 1.10` everywhere supports `H_NULL_NO_STABLE_MATERIAL_TTFT_HARM`.

**Stable but unexplained:** if parent-like `QF/SF >= 1.10` everywhere but no registered mechanism passes, classify `UNRESOLVED_STABLE_TTFT_HARM` and stop. No post-hoc hypothesis is added.

## Semantic and structural invalidation

Any failure/non-finite output, prefill dispatch census other than 441, submit count other than one, first-token disagreement across matched arms, or source/harness identity drift invalidates timing.

## Infrastructure budget

One infrastructure-only repair is allowed. It may repair orchestration only. After a valid repair the complete fresh identification collection must replay from zero. A second F0/infrastructure failure yields `STOP_INFRASTRUCTURE_UNSTABLE`.

## Intervention boundary

Identification does not automatically authorize optimization. If a supported mechanism exists, at most one mechanism-specific intervention can later be specified. There is no intervention tournament.

Any later intervention must preserve, with fresh evidence: exact semantics; decode ratio >=1.10; E2E ratio <=0.90; and TTFT ratio <1.10. Parent P6 observations cannot satisfy those future gates.

## Parent remains closed

Nothing in this program can change `ANL64_PROGRAM_CLOSED_VALID_NEGATIVE_TTFT_BLOCKED`. A future result is new evidence under a new protocol, not a retroactive ANL64 PASS.
